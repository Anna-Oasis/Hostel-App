DECLARATION AND UNDERTAKING

  • Resident will occupy only the room allotted and shall not change the allotted room without obtaining prior permission from the hostel authorities.
  • Every resident shall carry a valid University / Hostel Identity Card at all times.
  • Residents shall strictly follow the prescribed dress code.
  • Smoking, alcohol and narcotic substances are strictly prohibited inside the hostel premises.
  • Entering the hostel under the influence of alcohol or drugs is prohibited.
  • Residents shall return to the hostel before the prescribed reporting time and mark attendance daily.
  • Violation of attendance rules may result in disciplinary action including expulsion from the hostel.
  • Rooms and common areas shall be maintained neat and clean.
  • Residents shall keep their rooms locked while leaving and shall not hand over room keys to others.
  • Switch off lights, fans, AC and other electrical appliances when not in use.
  • Students are responsible for the safety of their personal belongings.
  • Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited.
  • Birthday celebrations and gatherings inside hostel rooms are not permitted.
  • Hostel property shall not be damaged or misused.
  • Visitors are not permitted inside hostel rooms without permission.
  • Residents shall follow the mess timings and avoid food wastage.
  • Residents shall maintain discipline and treat hostel staff and fellow students with respect.
  • Use of personal electrical appliances such as iron boxes, heaters and wireless routers is prohibited.
